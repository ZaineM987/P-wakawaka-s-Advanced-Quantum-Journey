Educator Notes: Quantum & Pedagogy

This application integrates 50 Core Quantum Computing Concepts designed to gently introduce the foundations of quantum physics and advanced computing to 4-to-6-year-olds.

It utilizes Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) by John Sweller to make advanced concepts accessible without causing fear or confusion:

  • Minimizing Extraneous Load: The vertical flex-column scroll layout enforces safe mobile tactile behavior. A massive spacer guarantees bottom options are always accessible.
  • Managing Intrinsic Load: Highly complex ideas like Superposition, Entanglement, and Interference are translated into ECE-friendly metaphors (spinning coins, magic twins connected by strings, splashing water waves).
  • Modality Effect: Text-to-Speech (TTS) supports pre-readers, moving load to the auditory channel.
